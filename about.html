🌱 Our Story

Making Sustainability Social & Accessible

We believe the climate crisis can only be solved when communities come together. GreenRadius is the tool to make that happen, one neighborhood at a time.

Our Mission

GreenRadius exists to solve a simple problem: sustainable living is lonely.

You might recycle diligently, bike to work, and reduce your water usage — but if you don't know if anyone else in your building or on your street is doing the same, it feels pointless.

We're building a platform that makes sustainable living visible, social, and rewarding. When you see your neighbors taking action, when you compete together on leaderboards, when you share tools instead of everyone buying their own — suddenly, individual actions become collective impact.

"We don't need a handful of people doing sustainability perfectly. We need millions of people doing it imperfectly, together."

The Problem We're Solving

Individual action isn't enough. Community action is the answer.

😔

The Loneliness Problem

73% of people who try to live sustainably feel like they're doing it alone. Without community support, they give up.

🤷

The Visibility Problem

Your neighbors might be composting, biking, and reducing waste — but you'd never know. Good behavior is invisible.

📉

The Motivation Problem

When you can't see the impact of your actions, it's hard to stay motivated. Abstract benefits don't drive behavior change.

Our Approach

We combine behavioral science, community design, and gamification

Hyperlocal Focus

We don't try to save the world. We focus on your specific neighborhood, building, or street. When you know the people you're competing with and collaborating with, you're more likely to act.

Research shows:

Peer influence is 2.5x more effective than financial incentives at driving sustainable behavior.

Gamification That Works

Points, badges, and leaderboards aren't gimmicks — they're proven tools for behavior change. We use them thoughtfully to make sustainable actions feel rewarding.

Our approach:

Short feedback loops, social recognition, and tangible rewards from local businesses.
