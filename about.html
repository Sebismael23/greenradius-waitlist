🌍 Our Story

Building a Greener Future Together

We believe that small actions, multiplied by millions, can transform our planet. GreenRadius is our answer to making sustainability accessible, engaging, and community-driven.

Our Mission

Making Sustainable Living Social & Fun

The climate crisis can feel overwhelming. We're often told that individual actions don't matter, that the problem is too big for us to solve. We refuse to believe that.

GreenRadius is built on a simple truth: when people work together, incredible things happen. By turning sustainable actions into a shared experience, with neighbors, friends, and communities, we can create a movement that scales.

Our mission is to make every person feel empowered to make a difference, one challenge at a time.

🌱

"Small actions × Community = Big Impact"

Behavioral Science

Our challenges are designed using proven behavioral psychology principles: habit stacking, social proof, and gamification. We make the sustainable choice the easy choice.

Hyper-Local Focus

Real Impact Tracking
